I lasted eleven days. Here is everything I learned, ranked by how annoyed it made me.

1. **The alarm isn't the hard part.** Going to bed earlier is the hard part.
2. **Tea at 5 a.m. is a different drink.** Quieter. Almost shy.
3. **The city sounds different.** Birds first, then the first rickshaws, then everything.
4. **I'm not a morning person.** But I'm someone who can *visit* mornings, which is more useful.

## The honest summary

I didn't become a new person. I did get a handful of very good quiet hours, and a reason to look out of the window more often.

Would I do it again? Probably — for a week, with better tea.
