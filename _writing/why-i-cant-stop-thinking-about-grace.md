There's a scene in *Grace* where nothing happens for four minutes: a woman waits at a bus stop in the rain, and the camera doesn't blink. I've thought about it every day since.

## What it's about

On paper: a woman returns to her hometown to sell a house. In practice: a long, patient study of what we do with our hands when we don't know what to say.

## What stayed with me

The sound design does most of the talking. Rain, a kettle, a door that sticks. By the end, I could hear the house when I closed my eyes.

> The best films leave a draft under the door. You notice it hours later.
