I painted this after a week of grey skies, when I realised I'd been photographing the rain instead of looking at it. I wanted a painting that felt the way *waiting for rain* feels: warm at the edges, heavy in the middle.

The palette came first — coral, lilac, a stubborn yellow, and a sky-blue I almost left out. Everything else was negotiation.

## A few notes

- The big circle was never planned. It started as a mistake in the sketch and I kept it.
- The yellow is the only colour I never mixed. It comes straight from the tube.
- I'm still not sure which way is up. I've hung it both ways.

There's a photo series from the same week if you want to see where my head was: {% include link.html to="_photography/rainy-days.md" text="Rainy Days" %}.
